📚 Student Lessons

Lesson 1: Introduction to ICT

ICT means Information and Communication Technology. It includes digital tools such as computers, phones and the internet that help people create, store and share information.

Lesson 2: Computer Hardware and Software

Hardware refers to the physical parts of a computer, such as the keyboard, screen and mouse. Software refers to the programs and instructions that run on a computer.

Lesson 3: Internet Safety

Keep passwords private, use trusted websites, think before sharing personal information and tell a trusted adult or teacher if something online makes you uncomfortable.
